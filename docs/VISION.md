# Umhlaba Wami — Product Vision & Idea Bank

This document captures the long-term product vision and a living collection of ideas. It is intentionally expansive so we can select, prioritise, and sequence the highest-impact capabilities as we move through the phases defined in `ROADMAP.md`.

---

## 1. Core Product Thesis

Commercial property in Eswatini (and similar markets) still runs on a fragmented mix of spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, paper leases, and personal relationships. Umhlaba Wami replaces that fragmentation with a single, trusted digital operating system that serves every stakeholder in the value chain:

- **Landlords / Asset Owners** — visibility, control, and higher net operating income
- **Property Managers** — operational command centre
- **Maintenance & Facilities Teams** — clear work queues and accountability
- **Finance Teams** — clean data and faster closes
- **Tenants / Retailers** — transparent service and easy self-service
- **Prospective Tenants** — modern way to discover and secure space
- **Platform Operator (Super Admin)** — healthy multi-tenant SaaS business

---

## 2. Experience Principles

- **Calm competence** — The interface should feel professional, fast, and never chaotic.
- **Local soul, global craft** — Place names, currency, and business culture of Eswatini; interaction design and reliability of the best global products.
- **One source of truth** — Every stakeholder sees the same underlying facts (with appropriate permissions).
- **Actionable by default** — Dashboards and lists should make the next correct action obvious.
- **Mobile-ready reality** — Technicians and managers are often on their feet; the product must work well on phones.

## 4. What “World-Class” Means Here

- **Reliability** — Trusted with money, legal documents, and emergency response.
- **Speed** — Core actions feel instant; lists and dashboards stay fast.
- **Clarity** — A new property manager productive within a day.
- **Depth** — Power users need not export to Excel to finish the job.
- **Polish** — Typography, empty states, errors, accessibility.
- **Local relevance** — Emalangeni, +268, Eswatini centre realities.

**Assessment (Phases 1–7 demo product):** Meets or exceeds the *product surface* implied by this vision for an Eswatini-first commercial OPS+marketplace. **Does not yet meet** world-class *production* reliability (shared backend, real payments, real messaging) until [OWNER_SETUP.md](./OWNER_SETUP.md) is completed.

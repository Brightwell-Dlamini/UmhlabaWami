## Phase 7 — Elevate ✅ Complete

Unified **Elevate (P7)** command centre implementing:

- **A** Photos, critical alerts  
- **B** Payments, invoices/Sage, renewals, CAM  
- **C** Field jobs, QR units, vendor scores, handovers, assets  
- **D** Predictive ops, pricing assist, NL ops, board narrative, benchmarks  
- **E** CSAT

- **G** POPIA, access review, DR checklist

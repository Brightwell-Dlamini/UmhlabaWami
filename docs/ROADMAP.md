# Umhlaba Wami — Product Roadmap & Phased Delivery Plan

**Vision Statement**  
Umhlaba Wami will become the definitive digital operating system for commercial real estate in the Kingdom of Eswatini.

## Phase 7 — Elevate (Next)

System-wide improvement — not a single feature, but depth across the whole OS:

1. **Production spine** — real Supabase, photos, realtime, email/SMS critical alerts  
2. **Cash & leases** — invoices, renewals, recoveries, payment rails  
3. **Field power** — tech mobile workflow, QR units, assets, CSAT  
4. **Intelligence & API** — predictive PM, board-pack narrative, live partner API

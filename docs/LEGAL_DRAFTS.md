## Privacy notice (outline)

1. **Who we are** — Legal entity name, contact, {{support_email}}.  
2. **What we collect** — Account data, property/tenant ops data, tickets, payments metadata, logs.  
3. **Why** — Provide the platform, security, billing, legal obligations.  
4. **Lawful basis** — Contract, legitimate interests, consent where required (POPIA).

7. **Your rights** — Access, correction, deletion requests via in-app POPIA tools / {{support_email}}.  
8. **Security** — Access control, encryption in transit, org isolation.
